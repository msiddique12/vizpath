# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level or background.

## Standards

Examples of positive behavior:
- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome newcomers and different viewpoints.
- Provide constructive feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Threats or intimidation.

## Enforcement

Violations should be reported to the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed promptly.

Contributors who do not follow this code may be asked to stop the behavior or be removed from the project.
